## Acceptance checks
- The full output renders reproducibly at the required settings.
- A frame-by-frame or timestamped review checks clipping, timing, captions, and audio sync.
- The reduced-motion version preserves the message.
- All source and generated assets have documented provenance and permission.
- Transcription is reviewed when accuracy matters; model or service availability is not assumed.
