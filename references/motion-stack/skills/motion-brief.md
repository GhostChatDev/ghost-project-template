# Motion brief

- Write the feeling in five words.
- Choose one motion idea. Do not animate five things at once.
- Replace every adjective with a number. “At least 170 px wide at 1080p” is allowed; “big” is not.
- Use one clock. If BPM is used: frames per beat = fps × 60 / BPM. At 60 fps and 120 BPM, a beat is 30 frames.
- Name the spring. Florence’s playbook says damping 1.0 lands dead, 0.8 hops once, and 0.5 bounces. Do not write “bouncy” or “natural.”
- UI motion is 150–300 ms. One entrance may be 400–800 ms. Ease out. No bounce on every element.
- For `prefers-reduced-motion`, use almost no motion while keeping information and controls.
- Write timing before animation.
- Done means: the loop closes, subject size passes, cold seek matches sequential seek, and sound hits one frame after the picture if there is sound.
- Unless `DESIGN.md` asks for them, ban purple gradient, glow orbs, fade-in on every element, a second type scale, cards nested in cards, and a rounded icon tile above every heading.
- Plan cheap, build once. Florence’s playbook prefers planning on Medium and building on High; do not use Max effort just because the film is long.
