# Product definition

Copy this file to `PRODUCT.md` at the start of a project. Replace the prompts with project-specific answers. Remove prompts that do not apply, but keep decisions that later work must follow.

## Project snapshot

- **Working name:** What is the project called?
- **One-sentence description:** What is it, in plain language?
- **Project type:** Is it an app, a game, or something else?
- **Current stage:** Is this a prototype, an MVP, or an established product?

## Audience

- **Primary audience:** Who is this for? Include relevant experience, needs, and context.
- **Secondary audience:** Who else might use it, and how do their needs differ?
- **User goal:** What is the main problem users want to solve, or the experience players want to have?
- **Assumptions to avoid:** What should the project not assume about its audience's knowledge, equipment, abilities, or availability?

## Purpose and scope

- **Core purpose:** What outcome or experience matters most?
- **Primary journey:** What should a user or player be able to do from start to finish?
- **Success looks like:** What observable result would show that the project serves its purpose?
- **In scope now:** Which screens, systems, or capabilities belong in the current milestone?
- **Out of scope:** What should not be built yet?
- **Open questions:** Which product decisions still need an answer?

## Platform and environment

- **Target platforms:** Which operating systems, devices, browsers, consoles, or storefronts are supported?
- **Form factors:** Which screen sizes, orientations, resolutions, and input methods matter?
- **Technology constraints:** Are there required languages, frameworks, engines, existing services, or version limits?
- **Offline and connectivity:** What must work offline, under poor connectivity, or after an interruption?
- **Accessibility:** Which accessibility needs and platform conventions must be supported?
- **Data and privacy:** What information is stored, where is it stored, and what must remain private?

## Things that must not change

List explicit constraints here so future work does not accidentally alter them. Examples are prompts only; delete any that do not apply.

- **Product behavior:** Which rules, workflows, mechanics, or outcomes are fixed?
- **Scope boundaries:** What must not be added or removed without approval?
- **Compatibility:** Which platforms, data formats, APIs, saved data, or existing behavior must remain compatible?
- **Content and tone:** Which terminology, ratings, themes, or content boundaries are required?
- **Brand or visual constraints:** Which established assets or identity elements must be preserved?
- **Technical or operational limits:** Which dependencies, costs, performance budgets, permissions, or external services are fixed?
- **Approval required for:** Which changes always need review first?

## Notes and source material

Link or list any briefs, references, research, decisions, or assets that provide additional context. Record the date and owner of important decisions when useful.
